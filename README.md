# 🚀 SkillBridge — AI-Powered Skill-Market Alignment Platform

> Bridging the gap between what India teaches and what industry needs.

**Smart India Hackathon 2026 | Problem Statement ID: SIH26134**

---

## 📌 Overview

SkillBridge is an AI-powered platform that identifies skill gaps between academic curricula and industry requirements. Only **41%** of trained graduates in India get placed — we fix this by comparing what colleges teach with what companies need, in real-time.

---

## ✨ Features

- **📊 AI Gap Analysis** — Upload syllabus → get Gap Score + Matched/Missing Skills
- **📝 Auto Quiz Generator** — Generates MCQs from uploaded materials
- **🔍 Job Market Scanner** — Real-time skill demand by location & role
- **🌙 Dark Mode** — Professional government-themed UI
